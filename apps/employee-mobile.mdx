The ChefOS Employee Mobile App puts your schedule, shift trades, time-off requests, and team messages right in your pocket. This guide walks you through everything you can do from the app.

## My Schedule

When you open the app, you'll see your upcoming shifts for the week. Each shift shows:

- **Date and day of the week**
- **Start and end time**
- **Position** you're working (e.g., Line Cook, Prep Cook)
- **Station** assignment, if applicable (e.g., Grill, Saut&eacute;)

Use the arrows at the top to move between weeks. You can look ahead to see future schedules as soon as your manager publishes them.

### Published vs. Draft Schedules

Your manager builds the schedule in draft mode before making it official. You will only see shifts once the schedule has been **published**. If you don't see shifts for an upcoming week, it means the schedule hasn't been published yet -- check back later or ask your manager.

When a new schedule is published, you'll receive a notification so you know to check your shifts.

### Confirming Your Schedule

After viewing a published week, you can tap **Confirm** to let your manager know you've seen and acknowledged your shifts. This helps managers track who's checked the schedule.

## Time-Off Requests

Need a day off? You can submit a request right from the app.

### Submitting a Request

1. Go to the **Time Off** section.
2. Pick your **start date** and **end date**.
3. Add a **reason** (optional but helpful for your manager).
4. Submit your request.

Your request will show as **Pending** until your manager reviews it.

### Partial-Day Requests

If you only need part of a day off (for a doctor's appointment, for example), toggle the **Partial Day** option and set the specific **start time** and **end time** you need off.

### Blackout Dates

Some dates may be blocked by management -- for example, holidays or big events when all hands are needed. The app will show you these blocked dates so you know not to request those days off. If you try to submit a request for a blackout date, you'll see a warning.

### Checking Request Status

Your time-off requests can have these statuses:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Waiting for your manager to review |
| **Approved** | You're all set -- enjoy your time off |
| **Denied** | Request was not approved (check for a note from your manager) |
| **Cancelled** | You cancelled the request yourself |

### Cancelling a Request

Changed your mind? You can cancel any **pending** request. Once a request has been approved or denied, only your manager can make changes.

## Shift Trades

The trade board lets you swap or give away shifts with your coworkers.

### Posting a Shift for Trade or Drop

If you can't work a shift, you have two options:

- **Trade** -- You want to swap your shift for someone else's shift.
- **Drop** -- You just want to give away the shift without getting one in return.

To post a shift:

1. Go to the **Trade Board**.
2. Pick the shift you want to trade or drop.
3. Choose **Trade** or **Drop**.
4. Add an optional message (e.g., "Happy to swap for any shift next week").
5. Post it.

### Picking Up a Shift

When you see a shift on the trade board that works for you:

1. Tap the shift to see details.
2. Tap **Pick Up** or **Offer Swap**.
3. If swapping, select which of your shifts you'd like to offer in exchange.
4. Add an optional message.
5. Submit your request.

### How the Approval Process Works

Shift trades go through a multi-step process to make sure everyone is on the same page:

1. **You post** your shift on the trade board.
2. **A coworker requests** to pick it up or swap.
3. **You accept** the coworker's request (or reject it and wait for other offers).
4. **Your manager approves** the trade (final step).

You'll get notifications at each step so you always know where things stand.

### Withdrawing or Cancelling

- If you posted a shift and no one has been approved yet, you can **cancel** the post.
- If you requested someone else's shift, you can **withdraw** your request before the poster accepts it.

## Open Shifts

Sometimes your manager creates shifts that aren't assigned to anyone yet. These are **open shifts** that any qualified employee can claim.

### Browsing Open Shifts

Go to the **Open Shifts** section to see what's available. You can filter by:

- **Date range**
- **Position** (to see only shifts for positions you're qualified for)

Each open shift shows the date, time, position, and station.

### Claiming an Open Shift

Found one that works for you? Tap **Claim** and optionally add a message to your manager. Your claim will go into a **Pending** state.

What happens next depends on your location's settings:

- **Manager approval required** -- Your manager reviews the claim and either approves or rejects it. You'll get a notification either way.
- **Auto-approved** -- If your location has auto-approval turned on and there are no schedule conflicts, the shift is yours immediately.

If the shift conflicts with one of your existing shifts, you'll see a warning and won't be able to claim it.

### Withdrawing a Claim

Changed your mind? You can withdraw a pending claim at any time before it's been approved.

## Availability

Your availability tells your manager when you're able to work each week. Keeping it up to date helps them build a schedule that works for everyone.

### Viewing Your Availability

Go to **Availability** to see your current weekly schedule. Each day shows the time window when you're available to work.

### Updating Your Availability

To change when you can work:

1. Go to **Availability**.
2. Tap on a day to edit it.
3. Set your **start time** and **end time** for that day.
4. Mark a day as **unavailable** if you can't work at all (for example, if you have classes every Friday morning).
5. Add optional **notes** to explain your availability (e.g., "Classes in the afternoon").
6. Save your changes.

Your manager will see your updated availability the next time they build the schedule. Updating your availability does not change any shifts that are already scheduled.

## Messaging

Stay connected with your team without leaving the app.

### Team Channels

Your location has shared channels where the whole team can communicate. These are great for announcements, questions, and general discussion.

### Direct Messages

Need to talk to someone privately? You can start a direct message with any team member:

1. Go to **Messaging**.
2. Tap the **New Message** icon.
3. Pick a person from the contact list.
4. Start chatting.

Messages are delivered in real-time, so you'll see responses as soon as they're sent. Message history is saved so you can scroll back through past conversations.

### Unread Badges

The messaging icon on the tab bar shows a badge when you have unread messages, so you won't miss anything important.

## Notifications

The app keeps you informed about schedule changes and other updates.

### What You'll Be Notified About

| Notification | When it's sent |
|---|---|
| **Schedule published** | A new week's schedule is posted |
| **Shift changed** | One of your shifts is added, moved, or removed |
| **Time-off decided** | Your time-off request is approved or denied |
| **Trade response** | Someone responds to your trade board post |
| **Trade resolved** | A trade is approved or denied by management |
| **Open shift claimed** | Your open shift claim is approved or rejected |

### Push Notifications

Enable push notifications to get alerts on your phone even when the app is closed. When you first open the app, you'll be asked to allow notifications -- we recommend saying yes so you don't miss schedule changes.

### Managing Notification Preferences

Not all notifications are equally important to you. Go to **Settings > Notifications** to turn individual notification types on or off. For example, you might want to keep shift change alerts on but turn off trade board notifications if you don't usually pick up extra shifts.

## Calendar Sync

Keep your work schedule in sync with your personal calendar so you never double-book.

### Connecting Your Calendar

1. Go to **Settings > Calendar Sync**.
2. Tap **Connect Calendar**.
3. Choose your provider: **Google Calendar**, **Apple Calendar**, or **Microsoft Outlook**.
4. Sign in and grant permission.

Once connected, your published shifts will automatically appear in your personal calendar as events. When your schedule changes, the calendar events update too.

### iCal Feed

If you prefer, you can export your schedule as an **iCal feed URL**. This works with virtually any calendar application:

1. Go to **Settings > Calendar Sync**.
2. Tap **Export iCal Feed**.
3. Copy the URL and add it as a subscription in your calendar app.

The feed updates automatically, so your calendar will always show your latest schedule.

### Disconnecting

To stop syncing, go to **Settings > Calendar Sync** and tap **Disconnect** next to the calendar account. All synced events will be removed from your personal calendar.

## Profile

Keep your contact information current so your manager can reach you when needed.

### What You Can Update

From **Settings > Profile**, you can edit:

- Phone number
- Cell phone number
- Street address, city, state, and ZIP code

Your name and email are managed by your manager and cannot be changed from the app. If they need updating, ask your manager.

## Certifications & Alerts

The app tracks important dates for your certifications, licenses, and required training.

### Viewing Your Certifications

Go to **Certifications** to see all your tracked items, such as:

- **Food Handler's Card** -- expiration date and renewal status
- **Alcohol Server Permit** -- expiration date
- **Required training completions** -- due dates

### Expiration Warnings

Items are color-coded by urgency:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Active** | Your certification is current -- no action needed |
| **Expiring** | Expiration is coming up soon -- time to renew |
| **Expired** | The certification has lapsed -- renew immediately |

You'll receive a notification when a certification is approaching its expiration date, giving you time to schedule a renewal before it lapses.
