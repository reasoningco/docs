---
title: "Messaging integrations"
description: "Connect team communication, channels, participants, and notification preferences."
---

Messaging integrations help operational systems notify the right people without leaving restaurant teams to monitor another tool.

## Common workflows

| Workflow | What to sync |
|---|---|
| Team channels | Location channels, department channels, and participants |
| Operational alerts | Messages tied to schedule changes, inventory issues, or sales exceptions |
| Notification preferences | User-level opt-ins, muted categories, and delivery settings |

## Recommended approach

Keep messages tied to a clear location, employee, or operational object so teams can act on them quickly.
