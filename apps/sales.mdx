---
title: "Sales integrations"
description: "Build POS imports, reporting, analytics, and performance workflows."
---

Sales integrations bring revenue and item performance into ChefOS, then make that data available for reporting and operational decisions.

## Common workflows

| Workflow | What to sync |
|---|---|
| POS import | Checks, tenders, line items, discounts, taxes, and service charges |
| Daily reporting | Net sales, gross sales, transaction count, average check, and category mix |
| Item performance | Menu items, quantities sold, revenue, margins, and sales mix |
| Forecast inputs | Historical sales by date, location, daypart, and category |

## Recommended approach

Use deterministic external IDs from the POS so imports can be retried safely without duplicating sales.
