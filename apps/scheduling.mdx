title: "Scheduling integrations"
description: "Build workflows for employees, shifts, availability, time off, and labor planning."
---

Scheduling integrations connect employee and labor workflows across ChefOS and external systems.

## Common workflows

| Workflow | What to sync |
|---|---|
| Employee roster | Employees, positions, contact fields, status, and home location |
| Schedule export | Published shifts, positions, stations, start/end times, and status |
| Availability | Recurring availability windows and exceptions |
| Time off | Requests, approvals, blackout dates, and request notes |
| Shift changes | Trades, drops, claims, approvals, and audit status |

## Recommended approach

Use read-only schedule access for calendars and dashboards. Request write access only when your system is the approved place to make schedule changes.
