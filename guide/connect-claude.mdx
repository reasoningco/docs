---
title: "Connect Claude"
description: "Use your ChefOS reports and records inside Claude, read-only, with your own ChefOS permissions."
---

The ChefOS connector lets Claude read your restaurant's records and reports and answer questions about them: sales, inventory, recipe costs, purchasing, waste and counts. It uses the Model Context Protocol (MCP), so it works in claude.ai, Claude Desktop, the Claude mobile apps and Claude Code.

Claude sees only what you can see in ChefOS: your organization, the locations you are granted and the areas your role can read.

## Before you start

| You need | Notes |
|---|---|
| A ChefOS account | The account you sign in to ChefOS with. |
| The connector enabled for your organization | Ask your ChefOS contact or write to [hello@chefos.us](mailto:hello@chefos.us). Until it is enabled, connecting fails with "the ChefOS MCP endpoint is not enabled for this organization". |
| A Claude plan that allows custom connectors | Free plans allow one custom connector. On Team and Enterprise plans, an owner adds the connector for the organization first. |

Your connector address is your organization's ChefOS address followed by `/v1/mcp`:

```text
https://<your-organization>.chefos.us/v1/mcp
```

## Add ChefOS to Claude

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open connector settings">
    In claude.ai, open **Customize** → **Connectors** and choose **Add custom connector**.

    On a Team or Enterprise plan, an owner opens **Organization settings** → **Connectors** → **Add** → **Custom** → **Web** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the address">
    Name it `ChefOS` and paste your connector address. Leave the OAuth client fields empty. If Claude asks how to register, choose **Register automatically**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and approve">
    Choose **Connect**. Claude opens the ChefOS consent screen. Sign in with your ChefOS account, check what Claude will be able to do, and choose **Connect Claude**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it in a chat">
    In a conversation, turn on **ChefOS** in the tools menu and ask a question.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The connector you add on claude.ai also appears in Claude Desktop, the mobile apps and Claude Code when they are signed in to the same Claude account.

## What Claude can do

| Area | What Claude can do |
|---|---|
| Analysis | Ask Sous, ChefOS's own analyst, which runs the reads, does the math in code and checks every number it states against the data. |
| Reports | Run the reports on the ChefOS **Reports** page: cost of goods, actual versus theoretical, purchases by vendor, category or item, price changes, transfers, count comparisons, recipe costs, product mix and sales. |
| Records | Query sales, inventory, recipes and costs, purchase orders, vendors, invoices, counts and line checks, then summarize or combine the results. |
| Locations | List the stores and regions you can see, with each store's current business date. |
| Inventory and purchasing | Read on-hand inventory and par levels, purchase orders and the products you can order from each vendor. |
| Draft purchase orders | Only if your organization turns drafting on and you can create purchase orders in ChefOS. A draft is never sent: someone on your team reviews and sends it in ChefOS. |

Claude cannot send, approve, receive or cancel an order, or change anything else in ChefOS.

## Example questions

- "What were net sales by store last week, and which store changed most against the week before?"
- "Show actual versus theoretical food cost for Harbor View this month."
- "Which items are below par at Downtown Central?"
- "Summarize purchases by vendor for September across all stores."

## Permissions and data

- **Your access, nothing more.** Claude acts as your ChefOS account. If an admin removes a location or a permission from you, Claude loses it on its next request.
- **Read-only by default.** The only thing Claude can create is a draft purchase order, and only where your organization allows it.
- **Usage limits.** Questions that use Sous count toward your organization's daily ChefOS AI limit.
- **Your data in Claude.** What Claude reads from ChefOS becomes part of your Claude conversation and is handled under your agreement with Anthropic. See the [ChefOS privacy policy](https://chefos.us/privacy) for how ChefOS handles your data.

## Disconnect

Remove the connector in Claude: open **Customize** → **Connectors**, choose **ChefOS** and remove it. Claude stops reaching ChefOS straight away.

## Connect Claude Code with an API token

Developers can connect Claude Code without the sign-in step, using a scoped ChefOS API token that an organization admin creates in Developer Access. See [Developer Access](/guide/api-access).

```bash
claude mcp add --transport http chefos https://<your-organization>.chefos.us/v1/mcp \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer <your-token>"
```

Give the token only the read scopes it needs, for example `sales.analytics.read` and `inventory.reports.read`.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "The ChefOS MCP endpoint is not enabled for this organization" | Ask your ChefOS contact to enable the connector for your organization. |
| You are signed in to the wrong ChefOS account | On the consent screen, choose **Switch account** and sign in again. |
| Claude says a result was cut to fit one response | Ask a narrower question: fewer stores, a shorter period, or one item or vendor. |
| "Reached today's ChefOS AI usage limit" | Try again tomorrow, or ask without Sous by naming a report. |

For help, write to [hello@chefos.us](mailto:hello@chefos.us). To report a security issue, write to [security@chefos.us](mailto:security@chefos.us).
